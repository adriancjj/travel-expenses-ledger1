How to use it for a group of 5

For every group expense, enter the payer’s name in Paid by, press Split cost, set People to 5, and enter the same five traveller names. At day end, choose that date here. Names must be spelled consistently for the calculation to match each person correctly.

How rates work

New payments use the current rate. Each saved payment permanently keeps the rate used at the time, so future rate changes never change your past settlement records.

The rate means: 1 unit of foreign currency equals X MYR. For example, USD 1 = MYR 4.6800.
